# Evidence ledger template

Use a ledger only while a task needs multiple investigations or specialists. Keep it
beside the active packet and delete or archive it at closure after moving durable facts
to the matching knowledge record.

| Time | Actor/role | Hypothesis or decision | Bounded evidence | Result | Next dependency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM | supervisor | One falsifiable statement | Path, command summary, or source | confirmed/rejected/open | Exact next check |

Rules:

- Record deltas, not chronological narration or full logs.
- Name which state the evidence establishes.
- Link specialist results by task ID.
- Record owner authority with exact scope; never include secrets or credentials.
- Close rejected hypotheses rather than retrying them without new evidence.
